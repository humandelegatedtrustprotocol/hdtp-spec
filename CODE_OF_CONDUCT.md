# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to build something good. Argue about the
work, never about the person.

This project's subject matter — private messages, identity keys, who is allowed to
reach whom — attracts people with strong opinions about security and privacy. Strong
opinions are welcome; contempt is not.

## What we expect

- Engage with what someone actually wrote, not the weakest version of it.
- Accept that a reviewer asking for evidence is doing their job. "It works on my
  machine" is not evidence; a failing test that then passes is.
- Say when you are wrong, fix it, and move on. Nobody here needs a long apology.
- Respect that maintainers' time is finite. An unanswered issue is usually a queue,
  not a snub.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments — including about someone's identity,
  background, or level of experience.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or persistent bad-faith argument after the point
  has been addressed.
- Using this project to attack people: code, issues, or documentation whose purpose is
  to target a specific person or group.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull requests, and any
channel that represents the project — and to conduct outside those spaces when it is
directed at someone because of their participation here.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **security@pact-protocol.com** with the subject
`[pact-protocol conduct]`. Reports are handled confidentially. You will get an
acknowledgment within 72 hours.

If your report concerns the maintainer, say so — you are entitled to expect that it is
not simply handed to the person it is about.

## What happens next

Depending on severity: a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban. Whoever handles a report explains the outcome to the reporter, and
recuses themselves if they are involved.

This project is aligned with the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
version 2.1; the text above is the project's own and governs where the two differ.
